# Cosmetic Brand Logo Design Spec

## Concept
A luxury 3D flower-shaped emblem designed for a premium cosmetics brand, replicating the metallic pink texture, glossy sheen, and soft lighting of the reference image.

## Composition
- **Symbol**: 6-petaled 3D abstract flower emblem with extruded depth, smooth rounded bevels, dynamic specular highlights, and soft floor drop shadow.
- **Typography**: Elegant serif brand title ("LUMIÈRE") with tracked sans-serif subtext ("PARIS • MAISON DE BEAUTÉ").
- **Background**: Neutral studio backdrop with radial lighting gradient to accentuate the metallic reflection.

## Palette
- **Metallic Pink Main**: `#F472B6`, `#DB2777`
- **Metallic Shadow/Extrusion**: `#9D174D`, `#700736`, `#4C0525`
- **Specular Highlights**: `#FFFFFF`, `#FBCFE8`
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` to `#E2E2E7`
- **Typography**: `#1C1917`, `#9D174D`

## Adherence to Reference & Request
- Directly matches the 3D glossy metallic pink texture, curved 6-petal flower motif, subtle reflections, and soft drop shadows present in the attached reference image (`eeeeeee.jpeg`).
